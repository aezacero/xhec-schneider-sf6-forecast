# Southeast Asia (Malaysia–Singapore corridor) · B2–B5

One of three Goal B markets (with the US and India), covering B2–B5. Every claim has a source link, or is marked **Logic** (our own reasoning) or
**Goal A** (our own data and models, with the repo file).

## Key terms
| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Corridor** | Johor (the southern tip of Malaysia) and Singapore, which sit next to each other and work as one data center market |
| **Hyperscaler** | A giant cloud company that builds its own data centers worldwide: Amazon (AWS), Microsoft, Google, Meta |
| **Colocation operator** | A company that builds data centers and rents the space to others (e.g. AirTrunk, GDS, YTL) |
| **MW (megawatt)** | How data center size is measured: the electricity its servers can use. 1 MW = 1,000 kW; 1 GW = 1,000 MW |
| **Vacancy** | Share of built data center space that is still empty and available to rent |
| **Pipeline** | Data centers that are planned or being built, not yet running |
| **TNB** | Tenaga Nasional Berhad, Malaysia's national electricity company; it runs the grid and connects every new data center to it |
| **kV (kilovolt)** | 1,000 volts. Medium-voltage switchgear works at roughly 1–36 kV; a home socket is 0.23 kV |
| **SF6** | A gas used inside older switchgear (SM6, RM6); it warms the climate about 24,000 times more than CO₂ |
| **AirSeT** | Schneider's switchgear without SF6 (uses pure air and vacuum instead) |
| **Scope 1 emissions** | Greenhouse gases a company releases directly from its own sites and equipment |
| **Net zero** | A target to cut emissions and offset the rest, so the total is zero |

|---|---|

| YTL Power doubled its green data center park in Kulai (Johor) to 1.2 GW (1,200 MW) | [NST](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/business/corporate/2026/06/1472220/ytl-power-speeds-data-centre-expansion-kulai-campus-doubles-12gw) |

**Logic:** Singapore has the customers and the money but little land and power; Johor has the space.
So a switchgear choice made by a company based in Singapore is often built in Johor. Low vacancy
means operators must build fast, and every new data center needs medium-voltage switchgear.

## B3 · Why customers would switch here, without any ban
**1. Being green already decides which data centers get approved.**
- Johor rejected about **30 % of data center applications** for not meeting sustainability goals
  ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2024/11/20/official-johor-snubbed-30pc-of-data-centre-bids-for-not-meeting-sustainability-goals/157396)),
  and a special committee checks each project's power, water and site
  ([Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/real-estate_1/malaysia-i-implementation-of-data-centre-framework-and-ii-initiative-for-sustainable-data-centre-development)).
- Malaysia's national guidelines ask data centers to track their carbon footprint, in exchange for
  tax incentives ([MITI guidelines](https://www.miti.gov.my/miti/resources/Media%20Release/Final_Guidelines_for_Sustainable_Data_Centre.pdf),
  [MIDA](https://www.mida.gov.my/mida-news/miti-finalising-sustainable-development-guidelines-for-data-centres/)).
- Singapore has Southeast Asia's first binding data center rules. Its last call only accepted
  projects with a **PUE of 1.25 or better** (PUE = total electricity used by the data center ÷
  electricity used by its servers; 1.0 would mean zero waste) and **at least 50 % green
  electricity** ([Eco-Business](https://www.eco-business.com/news/singapore-sets-southeast-asias-first-binding-data-centre-rules-building-demand-for-clean-energy/),
  [Withers](https://www.withersworldwide.com/en-gb/insight/read/singapore-s-second-data-centre-call-for-application-from-pilot-to-power-play),
  [IMDA Green Data Centre Roadmap](https://www.imda.gov.sg/how-we-can-help/green-dc-roadmap)).
- **Logic:** an operator fighting for scarce approvals and power has every reason to show clean
  equipment; switchgear without SF6 is a simple, visible part of that.

**2. The operators have their own climate targets.** AirTrunk and GDS aim for net zero by 2030 at
their Johor sites; DayOne signed up for 500 MW of renewable power; YTL's park runs on renewable
energy ([AirTrunk](https://datacenternews.asia/story/airtrunk-partners-with-pekat-solar-for-jhb1-solar-project),
[GDS](https://datacenternews.asia/story/gds-signs-21-year-renewable-energy-agreement-in-malaysia),
[Bernama](https://bernama.com/en/bfokus/news.php?id=2570321)). SF6 leaking from switchgear they own
counts in their own Scope 1 emissions (`Jessie_GoalB_research.md` §10).

**3. The rules are moving, and switchgear bought today will still be running when they arrive.**
- **TNB is testing switchgear without SF6:** ring main units (compact switchgear boxes) and
  "gas-free" hybrid units in Kuala Lumpur substations, and is considering lower-emission equipment
  for new installations ([TNB](https://www.tnb.com.my/sustainability/esg-stories-cleaner-grid-low-emission-switchgear)).
- **Singapore's carbon tax already covers SF6:** S$45 per tonne of CO₂-equivalent in 2026, rising
  to S$50–80 by 2030, for sites emitting over 25,000 tonnes a year ([NCCS](https://www.nccs.gov.sg/singapores-climate-action/mitigation-efforts/carbontax/)).
- **Malaysia** has announced a carbon tax for 2026 (first on iron, steel and energy); the rate is not
  published and the start date is being reviewed ([S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/energy-transition/102124-malaysia-sets-2026-carbon-tax-reaffirms-decarbonization-goals-in-budget-2025),
  [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/06/24/national-carbon-tax-policy-in-final-phase-says-mof),
  [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/my/en/assets/publications/2026/pwc-my-preparing-for-compliance-positioning-for-value.pdf)).
- Switchgear lasts 30–40 years (`Jessie_GoalB_research.md` §10): a unit installed in Johor in 2027
  is still running in 2060.

**4. The product fits the local grid.** Each switchgear is built for a maximum voltage:

|---|---|---|---|
| Malaysia (TNB) | Mainly 11 kV and 33 kV (22 kV and 6.6 kV in parts of Johor and Perak) | 11 / 22 / 6.6 kV: SM or RM AirSeT (up to 24 kV) fits. 33 kV: needs GM AirSeT (up to 36 kV); whether it is sold in Malaysia is to confirm | [TNB](https://www.tnb.com.my/sustainability/esg-stories-cleaner-grid-low-emission-switchgear), [TNB supply handbook](https://www.tnb.com.my/esah/supply-application/), [Schneider BE](https://www.se.com/be/en/product-range/21830291-sm-airset), [Schneider MY](https://www.se.com/my/en/product-range/21830291-sm-airset/) |

**Message for customers in the corridor:** *"Your approvals, your 2030 targets and the coming rules
all point the same way. AirSeT removes SF6 from your site for the next 40 years, at a raw-material
cost close to SM6."*

## B4 · Pricing and ways to win customers
**Price: close to competitors; a premium only where being green is scored.**
- **Goal A:** the raw materials in AirSeT cost about €75 more per unit than in RM6, about 0.6 % of
  the selling price (`pricing.md`, cost gap). Materials do not force a large premium.
- Data center operators (their approvals and targets depend on it): a modest premium, justified by
  support, monitoring and faster delivery.
- TNB tenders: decided mainly on price, against local makers; enter through TNB's pilot (B5), not
  by price-cutting.

**Levers**
| Lever | Why it works here | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Local assembly** with a licensed partner (as already done for 11 kV switchgear) | Lower cost and shorter delivery than importing, against local and Chinese makers | [Leapco](https://digitalmag.theceomagazine.com/advertorial-leapco/) (company advertorial) |
| **Speed: prefabricated modules** (built in a factory, installed on site) | Schneider says they cut a data center's build time to 6–8 months from about a year; with 0.7 % vacancy, being ready early is worth a lot | [Bernama](https://bernama.com/lite/news.php?id=2564298), [TechRepublic](https://techrepublic.com/article/news-data-center-johor-apac-malaysia/) |
| **Monitoring + service package** | AirSeT reports temperature, environment and breaker wear; Schneider's EcoCare service claims up to 40 % less maintenance; local training center in Kuala Lumpur | [SM AirSeT](https://www.se.com/my/en/product-range/21830291-sm-airset/), [Bernama](https://bernama.com/lite/news.php?id=2564298) |
| **Price-adjustment clause** (the price follows public copper and steel prices) | **Goal A:** our rules keep an AirSeT quote valid only 6 months vs 9 for RM6, because its raw-material risk is a bit higher (`pricing.md`); with the clause, Schneider can offer the longer period customers expect without taking that risk | `pricing.md`, `forecasting_other.md` |
| **Currency** | Quotes are in ringgit, Singapore dollars or US dollars, while metals are priced in US dollars. **Goal A:** our EUR/USD forecast range at +9 months is −5 % / +8 % (`forecasting_other.md` §3): state the currency and include exchange rates in the clause | `forecasting_other.md` |

## B5 · Roadmap, KPIs (measures of success) and risks
| Phase | Timing | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · First references (successful sites to show other customers) | 0–12 months | Win AirSeT on 2–3 Johor sites run by operators with 2030 targets (e.g. AirTrunk, GDS, YTL, DayOne) and on Singapore's new green projects (Jurong Island). With TNB: use the existing software relationship to offer AirSeT units for its SF6-free pilot, after passing TNB's product certification ([TNB certification scheme](https://www.tnb.com.my/doing-business-with-tnb/suppliers/our-requirements/product-certification-and-product-inspection-scheme)) |
| 2 · Make it standard | 12–24 months | Make AirSeT the default design for new data centers in the Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone (602 MW being built); scale up local assembly; follow TNB from pilot to its specifications for new installations |
| 3 · Extend | 24 months + | Reuse the references in Thailand (fastest-growing in the region, [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/southeast-asia-data-centre-investment-malaysia-johor-2031)) and Indonesia; cover 33 kV sites with GM AirSeT once it is available locally |

**KPIs**
- Win rate on data center tenders (offers accepted ÷ offers made), new vs existing customers.
- AirSeT share of Schneider's medium-voltage sales in the corridor, in units and in revenue.
- Number of reference sites (target: 3 by month 12); AirSeT units in TNB's pilot.
- Profit margin on AirSeT deals and quote validity achieved (link to Goal A).

**Risks: what could make it fail**
| Risk | Evidence | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Johor's boom slows (not enough power or water) | Moody's says the pipeline needs more power capacity; 8.5 GW announced vs 1.1 GW running; water approvals pending | Target projects already being built, not just announced ([Moody's](https://majujohor.bernama.com/news-en.php?id=2561319), [NST](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/business/economy/2026/08/1522642/johor-data-centre-boom-hits-capacity-wall)) |
| Cheap competition (local, Chinese) | CHINT wins on "cost performance"; TNB's agreement with State Grid's subsidiary | Local assembly; sell total cost over 40 years and speed, not the unit price |
| Rules arrive late (Malaysia's carbon tax delayed; no SF6 ban) | Malaysia is reviewing the tax start date | Lead with approvals and operators' own targets, which apply today |
| 33 kV gap | SM AirSeT goes up to 24 kV only; GM AirSeT's local availability unconfirmed | Confirm with Schneider; start with 11 / 22 kV sites |
| Exchange rates and copper prices | Copper +65 % in euros since Jan 2021, about +30 % (annualised) so far in 2026 (`eda_findings.md` §2, §5); metals priced in US dollars | Price-adjustment clause including exchange rates (B4) |
